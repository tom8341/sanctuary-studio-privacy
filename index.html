English

Effective September 3, 2026

GameAnalytics-enabled versions use GameAnalytics for limited, pseudonymous gameplay analytics. We do not collect names, email addresses, payment credentials, precise location, or advertising content through the Game. Optional Game Center features send leaderboard scores and achievement progress to Apple.

The individual developer identified as the seller on the Game's App Store listing operates under the studio name Sanctuary Studio ("we," "us," or "our") and provides Dungeon 9999m (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game.

1. Information we collect

We do not operate a game account system or a server that stores player profiles. Versions that include GameAnalytics use it to understand whether players can complete onboarding, begin and finish runs, reach progression milestones, recover after a defeat, and complete an optional in-app purchase. GameAnalytics may process:

  • a persistent analytics identifier based on Apple's Identifier for Vendor (IDFV), a session identifier, and session count;
  • device model, operating-system version, app and SDK version, connection type, event timestamps, and coarse location derived from the network connection;
  • product-interaction and gameplay events, including onboarding, run start/completion/failure, death, revive, restart, depth milestones, ending reached, difficulty, selected avatar, player level, death count, and run duration; and
  • purchase analytics such as product identifier, localized price, currency, purchase status, and a general failure reason.

Analytics events are associated with the persistent identifier so that sessions and retention can be measured. We do not intentionally send a player's name, email address, precise location, Apple Account credentials, payment-card information, raw StoreKit transaction identifier, or raw App Store receipt to GameAnalytics. Automatic Unity error submission, advertising events, and native crash reporting are disabled. The Game does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use the IDFA in the current configuration.

2. Information stored locally and in iCloud

The Game stores gameplay information locally on your device so that it can save your progress and preferences. When iCloud is enabled for the Game, save files are also stored in Apple's iCloud Documents and synchronized across supported devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The information may include:

  • game progress, achievements, inventory, and equipment;
  • settings such as language, audio, and control preferences; and
  • an installation-specific identifier used only to manage local save data.

This information is not transmitted to a developer-operated server and is not accessible to us through such a server. Apple provides and operates iCloud under your Apple Account settings. Apple's handling of iCloud information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Game Center

The Game optionally supports Apple Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the Game sends your deepest-depth leaderboard score and achievement progress to Apple. It may also receive your Game Center authentication status, leaderboard rank, and stored achievement progress so that these features work and remain synchronized across supported devices.

Apple associates Game Center activity with your Apple Account. Depending on your Game Center privacy and sharing settings, your nickname, avatar, scores, and achievement activity may be visible to other Game Center users. We do not receive your Apple Account credentials, and the Game does not send your Game Center player identifier, nickname, avatar, scores, or achievement progress to a developer-operated server or to GameAnalytics. Game Center is optional; you can manage sign-in and sharing under Settings > Game Center on your device. Apple's handling of this information is described in Game Center & Privacy.

4. In-app purchases

The Game may offer optional in-app purchases through Apple's App Store. An internet connection and an Apple Account permitted to make purchases are required to initiate and complete these transactions. Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment-card number or Apple Account credentials. The Game receives the purchased product identifier, transaction identity, receipt, and purchase status through Apple's StoreKit services only as needed to validate and deliver the purchased virtual item.

To prevent the same purchase from being delivered more than once, the Game derives a one-way SHA-256 hash from the StoreKit transaction identifier or receipt and stores that hash in the local save file. When iCloud synchronization is enabled, the hash is included in the save file synchronized through iCloud Documents. Raw StoreKit transaction identifiers and receipts are not stored in the save file or sent to GameAnalytics. A separate analytics event may report the purchased product, localized price, currency, and success or failure status, without the raw transaction identifier or receipt.

Apple's handling of payment and App Store information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party services

The Game is built with the Unity game engine, uses Unity In-App Purchasing to communicate with the App Store, optionally uses Apple Game Center for leaderboards and achievements, and uses GameAnalytics as an analytics service provider. Core gameplay can be played offline, but analytics, in-app purchases, Game Center, and iCloud synchronization require an internet connection. Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, GameAnalytics advertising integrations, automatic GameAnalytics error submission, and native crash reporting are disabled.

GameAnalytics processes analytics data on our behalf for product analytics, session measurement, security, and service delivery. Its current documentation states that technical logs and analytics may be retained for up to 12 months on a rolling basis and then deleted or permanently anonymized. For details, see the GameAnalytics Privacy Notice and Data Retention and Deletion Policy.

6. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect names, contact information, or other direct identifiers from children. GameAnalytics may process persistent identifiers for the internal operations described above. If you believe a child has provided information in a way that requires action, contact us using the details below.

7. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game data remains on your device until you erase the Game's data or uninstall the Game. Save data synchronized through iCloud remains in the user's iCloud storage until it is removed through the applicable device or iCloud storage settings. Scores and achievement progress submitted to Game Center are retained and managed by Apple under the player's Apple Account and Game Center settings. GameAnalytics applies its own retention schedule to analytics data as described above. To ask about analytics data access or deletion, contact us; we may need the GameAnalytics user identifier from the affected installation to locate a record.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game or its service providers change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date above.

4. 앱 내 구입

게임은 Apple App Store를 통해 선택적 앱 내 구입을 제공할 수 있습니다. 거래를 시작하고 완료하려면 인터넷 연결과 구입 권한이 있는 Apple Account가 필요합니다. 결제는 Apple이 처리하며, 당사는 결제 카드 번호나 Apple Account 인증 정보를 받지 않습니다. 게임은 구입 확인과 가상 아이템 지급에 필요한 범위에서만 Apple StoreKit을 통해 구입한 상품 식별자, 거래 식별 정보, 영수증 및 구입 상태를 받습니다.

동일한 구입이 중복 지급되지 않도록 게임은 StoreKit 거래 식별자 또는 영수증에서 단방향 SHA-256 해시를 생성하여 로컬 저장 파일에 보관합니다. iCloud 동기화가 활성화되면 이 해시는 iCloud Documents로 동기화되는 저장 파일에 포함됩니다. 원본 StoreKit 거래 식별자와 영수증은 저장 파일에 보관되거나 GameAnalytics로 전송되지 않습니다. 별도의 구매 분석 이벤트가 상품, 현지 가격, 통화, 성공 또는 실패 상태를 보고할 수 있지만 원본 거래 식별자나 영수증은 포함하지 않습니다.

Apple의 결제 및 App Store 정보 처리는 Apple 개인정보 처리방침의 적용을 받습니다.

5. 제3자 서비스

게임은 Unity 게임 엔진으로 제작되었으며 Unity In-App Purchasing을 통해 App Store와 통신하고, 선택 사항으로 Apple Game Center의 순위표와 업적을 사용하며, 분석 서비스 제공업체로 GameAnalytics를 사용합니다. 핵심 게임플레이는 오프라인으로 이용할 수 있지만 분석, 앱 내 구입, Game Center 및 iCloud 동기화에는 인터넷 연결이 필요합니다. Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, GameAnalytics 광고 통합, GameAnalytics 자동 오류 전송 및 네이티브 충돌 보고는 비활성화되어 있습니다.

GameAnalytics는 제품 분석, 세션 측정, 보안 및 서비스 제공을 위해 당사를 대신하여 분석 데이터를 처리합니다. 현재 문서에 따르면 기술 로그와 분석 데이터는 순환 방식으로 최대 12개월 동안 보관된 후 삭제되거나 영구적으로 익명화될 수 있습니다. 자세한 내용은 GameAnalytics Privacy Notice와 Data Retention and Deletion Policy를 참조하십시오.

6. 아동의 개인정보 보호

게임은 만 13세 미만 아동을 대상으로 하지 않습니다. 당사는 아동의 이름, 연락처 정보 또는 기타 직접 식별자를 고의로 수집하지 않습니다. GameAnalytics는 위에서 설명한 내부 운영 목적을 위해 영구 식별자를 처리할 수 있습니다. 조치가 필요한 방식으로 아동이 정보를 제공했다고 생각되면 아래 연락처로 문의해 주십시오.

7. 데이터 보관 및 삭제

기기에 로컬로 저장된 게임 데이터는 게임 데이터를 지우거나 게임을 삭제할 때까지 기기에 남아 있습니다. iCloud로 동기화된 저장 데이터는 해당 기기 또는 iCloud 저장 공간 설정을 통해 삭제할 때까지 사용자의 iCloud 저장 공간에 남습니다. Game Center에 제출된 점수와 업적 진행 상황은 플레이어의 Apple Account 및 Game Center 설정에 따라 Apple이 보관하고 관리합니다. GameAnalytics는 위에서 설명한 보관 일정에 따라 분석 데이터를 처리합니다. 분석 데이터 접근 또는 삭제에 관한 문의는 당사로 연락해 주십시오. 기록을 찾기 위해 해당 설치의 GameAnalytics 사용자 식별자가 필요할 수 있습니다.
