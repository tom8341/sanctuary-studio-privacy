Effective August 26, 2026

GameAnalytics-enabled versions use GameAnalytics for limited, pseudonymous gameplay analytics. We do not collect names, email addresses, payment credentials, precise location, or advertising content through the Game.

The individual developer identified as the seller on the Game's App Store listing operates under the studio name Sanctuary Studio ("we," "us," or "our") and provides 9999m: Down to the Princess (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Game.

1. Information we collect

We do not operate a game account system or a server that stores player profiles. Versions that include GameAnalytics use it to understand whether players can complete onboarding, begin and finish runs, reach progression milestones, recover after a defeat, and complete an optional in-app purchase. GameAnalytics may process:

  • a persistent analytics identifier based on Apple's Identifier for Vendor (IDFV), a session identifier, and session count;
  • device model, operating-system version, app and SDK version, connection type, event timestamps, and coarse location derived from the network connection;
  • product-interaction and gameplay events, including onboarding, run start/completion/failure, death, revive, restart, depth milestones, ending reached, difficulty, selected avatar, player level, death count, and run duration; and
  • purchase analytics such as product identifier, localized price, currency, purchase status, and a general failure reason.

Analytics events are associated with the persistent identifier so that sessions and retention can be measured. We do not intentionally send a player's name, email address, precise location, Apple Account credentials, payment-card information, raw StoreKit transaction identifier, or raw App Store receipt to GameAnalytics. Automatic Unity error submission, advertising events, and native crash reporting are disabled. The Game does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use the IDFA in the current configuration.

2. Information stored locally and in iCloud

The Game stores gameplay information locally on your device so that it can save your progress and preferences. When iCloud is enabled for the Game, save files are also stored in Apple's iCloud Documents and synchronized across supported devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The information may include:

  • game progress, achievements, inventory, and equipment;
  • settings such as language, audio, and control preferences; and
  • an installation-specific identifier used only to manage local save data.

This information is not transmitted to a developer-operated server and is not accessible to us through such a server. Apple provides and operates iCloud under your Apple Account settings. Apple's handling of iCloud information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases

The Game may offer optional in-app purchases through Apple's App Store. An internet connection and an Apple Account permitted to make purchases are required to initiate and complete these transactions. Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment-card number or Apple Account credentials. The Game receives the purchased product identifier, transaction identity, receipt, and purchase status through Apple's StoreKit services only as needed to validate and deliver the purchased virtual item.

To prevent the same purchase from being delivered more than once, the Game derives a one-way SHA-256 hash from the StoreKit transaction identifier or receipt and stores that hash in the local save file. When iCloud synchronization is enabled, the hash is included in the save file synchronized through iCloud Documents. Raw StoreKit transaction identifiers and receipts are not stored in the save file or sent to GameAnalytics. A separate analytics event may report the purchased product, localized price, currency, and success or failure status, without the raw transaction identifier or receipt.

Apple's handling of payment and App Store information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-party services

The Game is built with the Unity game engine, uses Unity In-App Purchasing to communicate with the App Store, and uses GameAnalytics as an analytics service provider. Core gameplay can be played offline, but analytics, in-app purchases, and iCloud synchronization require an internet connection. Unity Analytics, Unity Ads, GameAnalytics advertising integrations, automatic GameAnalytics error submission, and native crash reporting are disabled.

GameAnalytics processes analytics data on our behalf for product analytics, session measurement, security, and service delivery. Its current documentation states that technical logs and analytics may be retained for up to 12 months on a rolling basis and then deleted or permanently anonymized. For details, see the GameAnalytics Privacy Notice and Data Retention and Deletion Policy.

5. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect names, contact information, or other direct identifiers from children. GameAnalytics may process persistent identifiers for the internal operations described above. If you believe a child has provided information in a way that requires action, contact us using the details below.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game data remains on your device until you erase the Game's data or uninstall the Game. Save data synchronized through iCloud remains in the user's iCloud storage until it is removed through the applicable device or iCloud storage settings. GameAnalytics applies its own retention schedule to analytics data as described above. To ask about analytics data access or deletion, contact us; we may need the GameAnalytics user identifier from the affected installation to locate a record.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game or its service providers change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date above.

Esta Política de privacidad explica cómo se trata la información cuando utiliza el Juego.

1. Información que recopilamos

Las versiones anteriores no envían eventos a GameAnalytics. No gestionamos cuentas del Juego ni un servidor que almacene perfiles de jugadores. Las versiones que incluyen GameAnalytics lo utilizan para saber si los jugadores completan la introducción, comienzan y terminan partidas, alcanzan hitos, se recuperan tras una derrota y completan compras opcionales. GameAnalytics puede tratar:

  • un identificador analítico persistente basado en el identificador para proveedores de Apple (IDFV), un identificador de sesión y el número de sesiones;
  • modelo del dispositivo, versión del sistema operativo, versiones de la app y del SDK, tipo de conexión, marcas de tiempo y ubicación aproximada derivada de la conexión de red;
  • eventos de interacción y juego como introducción, inicio, finalización o fallo de una partida, muerte, reanimación, reinicio, hitos de profundidad, final alcanzado, dificultad, avatar, nivel, número de muertes y duración; y
  • análisis de compras como producto, precio localizado, moneda, estado y un motivo general de fallo.

Los eventos se asocian al identificador persistente para medir sesiones y retención. No enviamos intencionadamente a GameAnalytics nombres, correos electrónicos, ubicación precisa, credenciales de Apple, datos de tarjetas, identificadores de transacción de StoreKit ni recibos originales del App Store. El envío automático de errores de Unity, los eventos publicitarios y los informes de fallos nativos están desactivados. El Juego no solicita permiso de Transparencia de Seguimiento de Apps (ATT) y no utiliza el IDFA con la configuración actual.

2. Información almacenada localmente y en iCloud

El Juego almacena información de juego localmente en su dispositivo para guardar su progreso y sus preferencias. Cuando iCloud está activado para el Juego, los archivos de guardado también se almacenan en iCloud Documents de Apple y se sincronizan entre dispositivos compatibles que utilizan la misma cuenta de Apple. Esta información puede incluir:

  • progreso, logros, inventario y equipamiento del juego;
  • ajustes de idioma, sonido y controles; y
  • un identificador específico de la instalación utilizado únicamente para gestionar los datos de guardado local.

Esta información no se transmite a servidores operados por el desarrollador ni podemos acceder a ella mediante dichos servidores. Apple proporciona y gestiona iCloud de acuerdo con la configuración de su cuenta de Apple. El tratamiento de la información de iCloud por parte de Apple se rige por la Política de privacidad de Apple.

3. Compras dentro de la app

El Juego puede ofrecer compras opcionales dentro de la app a través del App Store de Apple. Para iniciar y completar estas transacciones se necesita una conexión a Internet y una cuenta de Apple autorizada para realizar compras. Apple procesa los pagos. Nosotros no recibimos el número de su tarjeta de pago ni las credenciales de su cuenta de Apple. El Juego recibe, mediante los servicios StoreKit de Apple, el identificador del producto, la identidad de la transacción, el recibo y el estado de la compra únicamente para validar y entregar el artículo virtual correspondiente.

Para impedir que una misma compra se entregue más de una vez, el Juego genera un hash SHA-256 unidireccional a partir del identificador de la transacción de StoreKit o del recibo y guarda dicho hash en el archivo local. Cuando iCloud está activado, el hash se incluye en el archivo sincronizado mediante iCloud Documents. Los identificadores de transacción y los recibos originales no se guardan en el archivo ni se envían a GameAnalytics. Un evento analítico independiente puede comunicar el producto, el precio localizado, la moneda y el estado de éxito o fallo sin incluir el identificador ni el recibo originales.

El tratamiento que Apple realiza de la información de pago y del App Store se rige por la Política de privacidad de Apple.
