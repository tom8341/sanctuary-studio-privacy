A short briefing from the developer.

Q1

What kind of game is Dungeon 9999m?

A portrait-format action RPG about fighting through an enormous dungeon and pushing your deepest record toward 9,999 meters.

Q2

What do I do during a descent?

Explore rooms, fight enemy groups, maintain kill streaks, collect weapon drops, manage potions, and keep moving deeper.

Q3

What makes progress visible?

The interface tracks your current depth, deepest record, remaining distance, level, health, magic, resources, and currency throughout the run.

Q4

Where can I play it?

Dungeon 9999m is currently available for iPhone through the App Store.
