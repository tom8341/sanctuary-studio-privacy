From the developer interview.

Q1

Do you remember what Dungeon 9999m was like at the very beginning?

It started as a game where you had to keep jumping down, fast.

Interviewer follow-up Why couldn't the player stop? Was something chasing them, or did you just want that feeling of having barely enough time to spot the next ledge before you had to jump?

I like that tension. I wanted players to keep facing new dangers.

Q2

Why 9,999 meters? What do you hope goes through players’ minds when they first see that distance?

That’s deep. Can I really make it?

Also, hell should be about 10,000 meters down, right?

I remember a legend about a Soviet borehole. They got down to around that depth, couldn't drill any farther, and supposedly heard the sounds of monsters in hell.
